## Exact calculation
  results = results of the most recent COMPLETED builds of this job, newest first, taken from the job's own Jenkins build history
            (Jenkinsfile walks currentBuild.previousBuild until 20 counted or history ends)
  counted: SUCCESS (success), FAILURE, UNSTABLE (not success).  skipped: ABORTED, NOT_BUILT, running (incl. the current build)
  rate = SUCCESS count / builds considered * 100      block when rate < 90
  < 20 builds available: all are used, reported as 'N builds available out of 20';  0 builds: explicit no-data warning (not 100%)
  Prometheus is NOT the data source: its build counters restart with Jenkins and hold no per-build data
  (measured: total_build_count_total=2 for taskflow-pipeline while Jenkins history has 61 builds) -> cannot give 'last 20 builds'.
